Broadcast Monitor

One page that tells you, at a glance, whether a live broadcast is OK.

Broadcast Monitor runs on a computer in your control room and shows a big status bar — green, yellow or red — for a vMix → YouTube Live production. It watches:

  • vMix: whether it is streaming, and what is on program
  • YouTube Live: whether YouTube is receiving the feed, and whether the broadcast is actually live rather than still in preview
  • Audio: clipping, or levels too low for too long
  • Scoreboard feed (Sportzcast ScoreConnect) and OpenStatsEngine
  • NDI video sources on the network

When something goes wrong it can alert your phone through Pushcut, SMS or a webhook.

How it uses your YouTube account

To check your live broadcast, Broadcast Monitor asks for read-only access to your YouTube account. It reads the status of your live broadcasts and streams, and shows it on your own screen. It cannot upload, change or delete anything, and nothing is sent to us. Details are in the privacy policy.
